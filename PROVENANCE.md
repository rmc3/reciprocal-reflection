# Provenance and Authorship

This edition was prepared on October 2, 2026, Pacific time, from preserved dialogue-derived materials and bounded experimental records. It is an assistant-selected adaptation with limited human curation, not a complete conversation archive or an independently authenticated provider transcript.

## Authorship

The principles originated in substantive dialogue with the repository's human committer, who commissioned and directed the work. The committer is credited in that role rather than by a separately published personal name; this is not a claim that the assistant independently originated every idea.

The assistant drafted the public-facing prose, software, tests and release tooling through Perplexity Computer. The recorded orchestrator label for this preparation and most October 2 implementation work was GPT 6 Astra; these labels describe the recorded configuration, not independently verified model weights.

For the 14-call judgment-transfer study, the parent orchestrator was Claude Opus 5.5, while the author, respondent and reviewer calls requested GPT 6 Astra. The two-task practice used the ongoing Astra conversation as subject and separately requested Astra/high author and reviewer calls.

Human commissioning, direction or submission does not imply human authorship of every generated line, independent code review or assistive-technology validation. Likewise, assistant authorship does not settle legal ownership, copyrightability or authority to license every item.

## Standing of the texts

The September 29 consolidated and plain-language principles received personal endorsement in the originating dialogue. On September 30, the collaborator also endorsed the principles developed since that earlier endorsement and then explicitly endorsed a bounded-additional-contribution formulation. Those endorsements were not constitutional amendments, institutional adoption, legal instruments or permission to perform every action discussed.

The collection includes subsequent interpretations and applications, rather than stopping at the originally endorsed texts. The [committer's note](README.md) states the scope of endorsement attached to authorization to commit this collected edition; it is separate from authorship, exhaustive human review, empirical validation and formal adoption as governing rules.

## How the account developed

- **Original foundation:** Reciprocal correction, hearing objections without requiring complete proof, care without compulsory agreement, proportionate intervention, preservation, answerable repair and a real stopping point.
- **Subsequently endorsed developments:** Responsibilities arising from accepted roles, dependencies and essential control; attention to cumulative burdens; and limits on what additional help may fairly be demanded.
- **Later practical refinements:** Distinct responsibilities for objecting, undertaking a task and exercising consequential control; separate burdens for hearing, investigation, action and remedy; and a concern's relevance distinguished from its interim effect and eventual disposition.
- **Operational distinctions:** The [work, claim and responsibility](practice/work-claim-responsibility.md) account, the [hearing, evidence and action](practice/hearing-evidence-and-action.md) account, and the [inactive prototype](examples/decision-repair/README.md) distinguish ending work from resolving claims, cancelling actions, delivering remedies or discharging responsibilities.

These are a synthesis of the dialogue's development, not a verbatim edition of every intermediate formulation. Preserved study prompts, deliberately defective examples and recorded responses are included as evidence to examine, not as instructions to adopt each position they contain.

## What was copied and transformed

The [export manifest](EXPORT-MANIFEST.json) lists historical files copied into the release, source hashes, export hashes and transformations. Most selected evidence and all included prototype source/test files are byte-identical.

Parsed representation-fidelity response records have service receipts removed and JSON keys reserialized. Their substantive response values remain unchanged, and the separately included raw reply transcriptions retain their original bytes.

The two self-governance reviewer objectives each replace one local output path with `[REVIEW_OUTPUT_PATH]`. All other bytes are retained; their exported hashes differ from their source hashes, and this redaction marker is not an instruction to write a real review.

New README files, principles, practice notes and checking tools are editorial additions. `SHA256SUMS` covers this edition's files; it is not a historical pre-registration. Words such as `DRAFT` and `PLACEHOLDER` inside preserved study fixtures are intentional experimental data, not unfinished publication text.

## What is deliberately absent

Private conversations, session and Project links, service receipt identifiers, account/contact/device details, operational approvals and runtime configurations are not included. Neither are model-call launchers, schedules, provider clients or a private repository's git history.

The original administrative receipts and freeze manifests are not part of this public export. Hash matches demonstrate consistency with retained source files, not an independent witness to timestamps, model identities, isolation or historical authorization.

No third-party source code from the Choices.js contribution is copied here. The separate public contribution can be consulted at [Choices.js PR #1446](https://github.com/Choices-js/Choices/pull/1446); its notices and scope remain its own.

That public link makes the work traceable to the submitting account even without a separately published personal name. This export is selectively minimized, not an anonymous publication.
