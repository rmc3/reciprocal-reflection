# Limitations

The repository combines normative proposals, experimental observations and implementation evidence. None of these automatically supplies the evidentiary support appropriate to the others.

## Principles and people

The principles are open to disagreement and are not presented as wholly novel philosophy. They do not establish a universal ordering of interests, an institutional right, machine experience or authority to represent others.

The dialogue and fictional cases are not affected-party participation. Naming the interests of absent people, animals or ecosystems does not establish their consent, a measured ecological benefit or a uniquely equitable priority.

## Studies

The studies use small, explicit tasks, substantial scaffolding and author-selected cases. Same-model advisory review is not independent corroboration, and context isolation was procedural rather than hermetic.

Null results apply to the tested comparisons, not every possible use of reflection. No stable model-weight update, broad transfer, counterfactual causal improvement or real-world benefit was established.

The export permits checking selected consistency and scoring claims. It does not reproduce hidden platform context, provider-side configuration or independent transcript attestation, and its checking scripts do not replace qualitative review.

## Code and validation

The prototype assumes a cooperative operator. It does not authenticate consent, evaluate evidence semantically, provide a review institution or control all possible action paths.

Passing fixture tests is narrower than production readiness. No security certification, comprehensive hostile-filesystem audit, Windows portability or live integration is claimed.

The release checker and study rechecker are newly authored tools. Their local passes support the specified checks, not a guarantee that the package contains no error or sensitive implication.

## Release and maintenance

This is a bounded reference snapshot with substantial LLM authorship and limited human curation. The committer's endorsement does not establish independent review or measured benefit, and no ongoing support, monitoring or experimental program is promised.

No person acquires an indefinite duty to maintain this work merely by collaborating with it, raising a concern or using the examples.
