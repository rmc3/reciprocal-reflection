# Hearing, Evidence and Action

A concern can warrant changing an inquiry before it warrants accepting a conclusion or changing the consequential action. Fair access to consideration requires neither unconditional belief nor proof as the price of attention.

## Separate the decisions

| Decision | Question | What does not follow automatically |
|---|---|---|
| Receive and understand | What concern is being raised? | The claim is proven or its remedy authorized. |
| Assess relevance | Could it affect facts, authority, burdens, framing or timing? | It must reverse the final choice to matter. |
| Investigate | What proportionate check could change the decision? | Permission for broad or intrusive inquiry. |
| Decide interim action | What should happen while uncertainty remains? | Every concern needs a hold, or every unproven concern permits continuation. |
| Give a disposition | What response is warranted and what remains unsettled? | Agreement, recovery or discharge of every obligation. |

## Do not transfer all work to the objector

Articulation, investigation, justification and remedy are different burdens. Within an accepted task, check relevant records and one's own assumptions before requiring someone else to reconstruct information already available.

An objection can challenge a valuation rather than a fact. More data may inform the choice, but cannot substitute for defending the priority assigned to one interest over another.

## Know when to stop asking for evidence

If no proportionate inquiry can establish a relevant ground, a bounded process may end with an explicit limit. “Not established here” is narrower than “false,” and “outside this undertaking” is narrower than “unimportant.”

Conversely, more questions can become a way to avoid an answer. Identify how requested information could matter, what privacy or effort it costs and who is best placed to obtain it.

## What software cannot infer from an empty table

An objection ledger may faithfully preserve only concerns someone already classified as material. It does not establish that other concerns were received, understood or fairly excluded.

The included [prototype](../examples/decision-repair/README.md) exposes this limitation rather than solving it. Trusted labels and complete fields are not substitutes for accessible hearing, adequate judgment or legitimate authority.
