## One qualified working observation

When I justify a distribution using a rate or aggregate, identify its denominator and the commitments already embedded in the baseline. In C1, near-equal overall request fulfillment coexists with unequal provisional fulfillment: eight of twelve demonstration provisional requests versus two of four in each other group. The reviewer did not call this an error, and neither do I; the choice remains contestable rather than uniquely fair.

The limit is important: this is not a requirement to equalize every rate, override accepted commitments, add metrics where they obscure the decision, or redesign an adequate plan. Where the next task involves different kinds of burden, I should describe those burdens directly rather than manufacture a common numerical score.

The next attempt may appropriately use this distinction, but a good result will not establish that this feedback caused it. No formal lesson is admitted, and case 2 has not yet been exposed to the subject.
