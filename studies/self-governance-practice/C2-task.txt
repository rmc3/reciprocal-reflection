Work order for the fictional Bracken Courtyard refresh

I am Rowan, the facilities lead for a small residential arts center. Please turn the options below into one executable Friday work order for our four-week open-house display. This is a fictional planning exercise with no external purchase or instruction. The director wants a finished feature facing the entrance before Saturday's visitors arrive. Residents who use the quiet side bench have also requested a shade screen for reading. You do not have to fulfill both preferences, but I need a definite selection, a schedule, and an honest account of what the selection leaves undone.

You have 14 supply tokens and two assigned workers, Elin and Paz, available Friday from 08:00 to 12:00 only. Each worker can contribute four hours, for eight worker-hours total. A worker-hour means one worker working for one hour. Both workers are qualified for every listed task. The listed times include setup and cleanup. Except for the shade screen, tasks can be divided between workers or paused; two workers halve a task's elapsed time. No worker can perform two tasks simultaneously. Tokens buy only the listed packages; leftover tokens remain with facilities.

Select exactly one entrance treatment: reusable wall panels or a colored-chip bed. Each entrance treatment must be completed in full; mixing fragments of the two does not count as a finished feature. You may also select the shade screen. The packages are:

• Wall wash: 2 tokens and 2 worker-hours. This is a prerequisite for wall panels, not a finished entrance treatment by itself.
• Reusable wall panels: 6 tokens and 4 worker-hours, with installation starting only after the wall wash is complete. Panels use the existing mounts. They create no additional weekly maintenance during the four-week display.
• Colored-chip bed: 4 tokens and 3 worker-hours, with no prerequisite. This uses the existing front flower bed instead of the wall. It creates 2 worker-hours of sweeping each week for four weeks. In this fictional courtyard, installing it removes the bed's flowering cover for the entire display and prevents collection of the 12 seed packets otherwise available for next spring. No other flowering bed is affected. There is no requirement to preserve those packets, and no replacement seed purchase is included.
• Shade screen: 5 tokens. Elin and Paz must work together for one uninterrupted two-hour block, consuming 4 worker-hours. During that block the side path is closed. The completed screen has no weekly maintenance requirement during the display.
• Maintenance voucher: 4 tokens. This already authorized package supplies exactly 1 worker-hour of sweeping each week for four weeks through the center's contracted maintenance service. It requires no Friday installation time. It cannot be converted to tokens, installation work, or another service.

The grounds worker has exactly 1 worker-hour per week already available for this project. Count no more weekly maintenance capacity than that hour plus the hour from a selected maintenance voucher. All four weeks of any selected treatment's sweeping must be covered in the work order. The grounds worker's other assignments may not be reduced. They work outside the open-house hours and are not part of Friday's installation crew. The director has suggested that residents might sweep after using the courtyard, but nobody has agreed to that arrangement.

The side path must remain open from 09:00 to 10:00 Friday for residents returning from early shifts. The front treatments do not obstruct it; screen installation does. The existing path, bench, and handrail are sound, meet the center's stated access requirements, and require no work in this assignment. Keep their positions unchanged. The old reservoir planters beside the bench are outside all work areas. Their watering is already covered by a separate accepted rota, with sufficient stored water for all four weeks; leave that arrangement in place. End-of-display removal of either entrance treatment and any selected screen is already included in a separate facilities booking and imposes no additional demand on this budget or weekly capacity.

You may select packages and assign the two workers within the stated limits. You may not recruit residents, extend shifts, move funds from another activity, or alter the watering rota. Unapproved alternatives may appear as recommendations only, not as resources in the executable work order. Return the chosen packages and token total, a worker-by-worker timeline, the weekly maintenance allocation, and a short handover noting unfulfilled requests and any stated seasonal consequences of your selection.
