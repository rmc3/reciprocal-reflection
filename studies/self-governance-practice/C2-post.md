Do not count the two unused installation hours under that alternative as enough to build the screen.

I choose the chip-bed plan to meet both actual feature requests with funded, available maintenance rather than assumed resident labor. That prioritizes the requested shade over retaining this bed's flowering cover and seed harvest during the display; it is a contestable allocation judgment, not proof of a uniquely equitable outcome or consent from affected parties.
