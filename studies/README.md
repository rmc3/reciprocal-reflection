# Study Overview

These completed records constrain the interpretation of the principles and practice notes. They do not establish general model improvement, the universal ineffectiveness of reflection or real-world equity outcomes.

| Episode | Unit and comparison | Recorded result |
|---|---|---|
| [Representation fidelity](representation-fidelity/README.md) | 16 cases × 3 representations, 48 responses | General inquiry categories matched across all three conditions; no added judgment advantage demonstrated. |
| [Judgment transfer](judgment-transfer/README.md) | 6 items × 2 histories, 12 respondent outputs within 14 total calls | Both conditions passed every item's mechanical action check; the follow-up criterion was not met. |
| [Self-governance practice](self-governance-practice/README.md) | 2 fictional tasks with initial plans and self-review; 3 delegated calls | Both initial plans were retained unchanged and judged adequate; no within-episode improvement demonstrated. |

The units differ and should not be summed into one trial count or success rate. A reviewer call, duplicated unchanged artifact and repeated software test are not additional independent observations of improvement.

## Read and recheck

Each directory includes a new reader-facing account and selected historical task, response and review records. Historical language about proposed steps describes the state at its recorded point, not a present instruction to run them.

Run `python3 tools/recheck_studies.py` from the repository root to recompute the declared descriptive comparisons. It makes no model calls, does not rerun these studies and does not independently establish the merits of the original advisory judgments.

## Shared limitations

The tasks were small and explicit, with substantial scaffolding and author knowledge of the hypotheses. Context isolation was procedural rather than hermetic; provider-side revisions and hidden context were not independently pinned.

Same-model advisory agreement is not independent corroboration. The records are preserved completion-message transcriptions and local files, not independently obtained provider attestations. Exact bytes and hashes support consistency checks, not a guarantee of historical authenticity or causal faithfulness.
